DEPOSITION OF JORDAN LEE HARRIS
Smith Manufacturing LLC v. Apex Logistics, Inc.
Case No. 3:24-cv-01842
Taken July 12, 2025

APPEARANCES:
For Plaintiff: Counsel for Smith Manufacturing
For Defendant: Counsel for Apex Logistics
Witness: Jordan Lee Harris, Director of Operations, Apex Logistics, Inc.

JORDAN LEE HARRIS, having been first duly sworn, testified as follows:

Q. Please state your full name for the record.
A. Jordan Lee Harris.

Q. What is your position at Apex Logistics?
A. I'm the Director of Operations. I've held that role since March 2021.

Q. Who do you report to?
A. I report to the COO, Patricia Okonkwo.

Q. Are you familiar with the shipment of industrial coolants under purchase order PO-4491?
A. Yes. That was a rush shipment scheduled for delivery to Smith Manufacturing on November 3, 2024.

Q. Did that shipment arrive on November 3, 2024?
A. No. It arrived on November 8, 2024.

Q. Why was it late?
A. There was a driver shortage at our Dayton hub. I was told about the shortage on November 1 by the hub manager, Marcus Webb.

Q. Did you notify Smith Manufacturing of the delay on November 1?
A. I believe customer service sent an email. I did not personally call anyone at Smith.

Q. Did you review Exhibit 12 before today, the email chain between Apex and Smith?
A. Yes.

Q. Looking at Exhibit 12, is there any email from Apex dated November 1, 2024 advising of a delay?
A. I don't see one dated November 1. There is an email on November 4 apologizing for the delay.

Q. So as of November 3, the original delivery date, Smith had not been told the load would miss?
A. Based on Exhibit 12, that appears correct. I don't independently remember a November 1 notice.

Q. What was the temperature-control requirement for PO-4491?
A. The coolants required refrigerated transport between 35 and 45 degrees Fahrenheit.

Q. Was the trailer used on November 8 a refrigerated trailer?
A. Yes. Unit R-208.

Q. Do you know whether the temperature log for R-208 on that trip stayed within range?
A. I have not seen a temperature log that confirms continuous compliance. Our system shows the reefer was set to 38 degrees, but I cannot testify that it never went out of range.

Q. Have you seen Exhibit 18, the temperature download for R-208 dated November 8?
A. I glanced at it this morning. It shows a spike to 58 degrees for about 40 minutes near mile marker 142.

Q. Did you investigate that spike?
A. Not personally. I asked fleet maintenance to check the unit. I never received a written report.

Q. Did Apex offer Smith a credit for the late delivery?
A. I recommended a 10% freight credit. I don't know if it was issued.

Q. Who would know whether the credit was issued?
A. Accounts receivable, or possibly Patricia Okonkwo.

Q. Prior to this lawsuit, did you destroy any emails related to PO-4491?
A. No. I followed the litigation hold I received in January 2025.

END OF EXCERPT
